01 — About

The journey & the Lonoup philosophy

I'm pnnnhan99 — a full-stack developer with a deep focus on backend architectures. My foundation is built on Laravel and Node.js, empowering me to build scalable platforms and deliver high-quality web development services.

Currently, I am architecting lonoup.com. It is designed to be more than just a portfolio; it is a central hub for professional web solutions and an expanding ecosystem of digital tools (like social.lonoup and studio.lonoup).

Alongside providing premium client services, I am passionate about integrating AI & Python into everyday tools, sharing knowledge through technical blogs, and maintaining open-source repositories for the community.
